2. Modern home at dusk, by Hamza Awan: https://www.pexels.com/video/17224730/
3. Luxurious living room, by TR Studio: https://www.pexels.com/video/29466011/
4. Softly lit bedroom, by Alef Morais: https://www.pexels.com/video/39024330/
5. Modern home from above at twilight, by Alef Morais: https://www.pexels.com/video/39024336/
6. Apartment towers at night, by Soumyadeep Das: https://www.pexels.com/video/31067264/

Each runs about 4 seconds with a 1-second cross-fade; the end fades into the start, so the loop
is seamless (24 seconds). Encoded with ffmpeg: H.264, no sound, 24 fps, `+faststart`.
`public/images/hero/hero-montage-first-frame.jpg` is its first frame, shown until the video plays.
